FAQs

Common questions from the Facebook group

Do I need to be an experienced builder to start a MiniMax?

No — the class is designed to be approachable for a first scratch-build. The plans and this community's build logs walk through each stage.

Is my exact address ever shown on the map?

No. Only the country, region and locality you choose to share are used to place your pin — never a precise address, and never without your consent.

Can I appear on the map without a public profile?

Yes. Registering only adds you to the fleet count and region stats; a visible pin with your name and photo is a separate opt-in.

How do I update my project status later (planning → building → sailing)?

Log in with the email you registered with and edit your entry at any time — no need to register again.

Is this site run by Castlerock Yachtworks?

Not yet — it's a fan-built proposal being put to the group admins. If adopted, ownership and hosting will be discussed with them directly.
